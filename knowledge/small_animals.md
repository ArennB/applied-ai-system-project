# Small animals and other pets

## Rabbits
Rabbits need daily unlimited hay, fresh greens, and fresh water: about 10 minutes
a day, high priority. They also need at least 3 hours a day out of the hutch to
exercise, and daily spot-cleaning of the litter area for about 5 to 10 minutes.
A full enclosure clean is weekly, about 30 minutes, medium priority.

## Guinea pigs and hamsters
Guinea pigs need daily hay, vegetables high in vitamin C, and fresh water; about
10 minutes, daily, high priority. Hamsters need daily food and water checks of
about 5 minutes, and a weekly cage clean of 20 to 30 minutes.

## Birds
Pet birds need fresh food and water daily (about 10 minutes, high priority), at
least an hour of out-of-cage time or interaction, and a weekly cage clean of
20 to 30 minutes.

## Fish
Feed fish once or twice a day; about 2 minutes. Check temperature and filter
daily. A partial water change of 10 to 25 percent is weekly and takes 20 to 30
minutes, medium priority.
