# Cat daily care

## Feeding cats
Adult cats usually eat two meals a day, about 12 hours apart. Each feeding takes
5 to 10 minutes including refreshing water and washing the bowl. Feeding is a
daily, high-priority task.

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box at least once a day; about 5 minutes. It is daily and high
priority, because a dirty box is a common reason cats toilet elsewhere. Do a full
litter change and box wash weekly, about 15 to 20 minutes, medium priority.

## Play and exercise for cats
Indoor cats need around 15 to 30 minutes of interactive play a day, ideally in
two sessions of 10 to 15 minutes with a wand toy. Play is daily and medium
priority, high for a young or overweight cat.

## Cat grooming
Short-haired cats need brushing about once a week for 10 minutes. Long-haired
breeds such as Persians and Maine Coons need brushing daily or every other day
for 10 to 15 minutes to prevent mats. Grooming is usually low priority for short
coats and medium priority for long coats.
