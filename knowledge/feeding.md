# Feeding

## Adult dog feeding
Most adult dogs eat two meals a day, morning and evening. Each feeding takes
about 10 minutes including preparing food, refreshing water and washing the bowl.
Feeding is a daily, high-priority task and should not be skipped to save time.

## Fresh water
Every pet needs fresh water every day. Refreshing water is usually folded into a
feeding task, but a pet on a kidney or urinary diet may need water bowls or
fountains checked separately; about 5 minutes, daily, high priority.

## Special diets
Prescription diets, weight-loss plans and allergy diets only work if followed
consistently. Measure portions rather than free-feeding. Preparing a special or
raw diet can take 10 to 20 minutes per meal.

## Feeding order and timing
Feed dogs either an hour before or about an hour after vigorous exercise, not
right before, to reduce the risk of bloat in large deep-chested breeds such as
huskies, great danes and German shepherds.
