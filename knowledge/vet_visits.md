# Vet visits and appointments

## Planning a vet appointment
A vet visit is a fixed-time, high-priority task: it cannot be moved by the
planner. Allow the appointment length plus travel; a routine check-up is usually
30 minutes of appointment and 15 to 30 minutes of travel each way, so 45 to 90
minutes in total. Record it as a one-off ("once") task at the appointment time.

## Before the appointment
Some appointments require fasting (no food from the night before), so a morning
feeding may need to move after the visit. Getting a cat into a carrier takes
5 to 10 minutes; leave it out the day before so it is familiar.

## Routine check-up frequency
Healthy adult pets should see the vet once a year; seniors every six months;
puppies and kittens several times in their first months for vaccinations.
