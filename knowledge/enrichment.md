# Enrichment and training

## Mental stimulation for dogs
Puzzle feeders, snuffle mats and scent games tire a dog out as much as a walk.
A 10 to 15 minute enrichment session is a good substitute when time for exercise
is short. Medium priority, daily for high-energy breeds.

## Training sessions
Short, regular training beats long, rare sessions: 5 to 10 minutes once or twice
a day. Medium priority for adult dogs, high for puppies and newly adopted dogs.

## Cat enrichment
Window perches, food puzzles and rotating toys keep indoor cats engaged. A
10 minute session hiding treats or setting up a puzzle feeder is a medium-priority
daily task, especially for cats left alone during the day.

## Alone time and anxiety
Dogs with separation anxiety benefit from a 10 to 15 minute calm walk or sniff
session before the owner leaves, and a predictable departure routine.
