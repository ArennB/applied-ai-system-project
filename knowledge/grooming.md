# Grooming

## Dog brushing
Double-coated breeds such as huskies, malamutes and German shepherds shed heavily
and benefit from brushing two or three times a week for 15 to 20 minutes, daily
during shedding season. Short-coated dogs need a 5 to 10 minute brush weekly.
Brushing is usually low to medium priority.

## Nail trims
Nails need trimming about every 3 to 4 weeks; about 10 to 15 minutes. Low
priority on a typical day, but do not let it slide for more than a month.

## Teeth cleaning
Daily tooth brushing is ideal for dogs and cats and takes about 2 to 5 minutes.
If daily is unrealistic, aim for several times a week. Medium priority.

## Bathing dogs
Most dogs only need a bath every 4 to 8 weeks, or when dirty; 20 to 40 minutes
including drying. Low priority, a weekly or once task at most.
