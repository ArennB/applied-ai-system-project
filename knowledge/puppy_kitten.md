# Puppies and kittens

## Puppy exercise
Puppies need short, frequent activity rather than long walks. A common rule of
thumb is about 5 minutes of structured walking per month of age, up to twice a
day: a 4-month-old puppy gets walks of about 20 minutes. Free play in the garden
or home does not count toward that limit.

## Puppy toilet breaks and training
Young puppies need a toilet break after every meal, nap and play session, and
roughly every 2 hours while awake. Training sessions should be short: 5 to 10
minutes, two or three times a day, medium to high priority while the puppy is
learning house rules.

## Puppy and kitten feeding
Puppies and kittens under six months usually eat three or four small meals a day.
Each feeding takes about 5 to 10 minutes including cleanup and is high priority
and daily.

## Kitten play and socialisation
Kittens need two or three interactive play sessions a day of 10 to 15 minutes to
burn energy and learn bite inhibition. Handling paws, ears and mouth for a few
minutes a day makes later grooming and vet visits easier.
